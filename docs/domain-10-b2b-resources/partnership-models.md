# B2B Partnership Models: Distributor, OEM & ODM

Migibio partners with businesses through three models. This page explains each and how to choose the right one for your market.

## The Three Models

| Model | What it means | Best for |
|-------|---------------|----------|
| **Distributor** | Resell Migibio-branded products | Established distribution networks |
| **OEM** | Your brand on Migibio-manufactured products | Building your own brand |
| **ODM** | Migibio designs + manufactures to your spec | Customized product requirements |

## Choosing a Model

| Situation | Recommended model |
|-----------|-------------------|
| You have a sales network but no brand | Distributor |
| You want your own brand quickly | OEM |
| You need custom specifications | ODM |

## What Migibio Provides

- **Technical support** — application and clinical training.
- **Documentation** — IFU, COA, validation reports.
- **Regulatory support** — registration assistance per market.
- **Marketing collateral** — technical content, this knowledge base.
