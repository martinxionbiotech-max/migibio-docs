# Manufacturing & Production

Migibio manufactures its own fluorescence immunochromatographic (FICT) test strips and analyzers end-to-end. This domain documents *how* products are made — the facility, the equipment, the raw materials, and the quality gates that every lot passes before it ships.

## The Manufacturing Facility

| Item | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Location | Guangzhou Science City, Huangpu District, Guangzhou, China |
| Area | 2,000 m² standardized R&D & production workshop |
| Certification | ISO9001 (quality management) + GMP (good manufacturing practice) |
| Environment | Controlled temperature & humidity with industrial-grade rotary dehumidification |

## Core Production Equipment

| Equipment | Function | Quality impact |
|-----------|----------|----------------|
| Automatic laminating machine | Assembles membrane, conjugate pad, and sample pad into a continuous card | Consistent alignment, no manual variance |
| Continuous film stripper (dispenser) | Dispenses T-line/C-line reagents and conjugate onto membrane | Uniform line width & volume — directly affects CV% |
| High-speed strip cutter | Cuts laminated cards into individual 3–5 mm strips | Precise strip width, clean edges |
| Industrial rotary dehumidification | Maintains low-humidity production environment | Protects hygroscopic reagents and membrane |

## The Production Workflow (Overview)

```
Raw material IQC → Membrane coating → Drying → Conjugate pad prep → Lamination
→ Cutting → Cassette assembly & pouching → Labeling → FQC/OQC → Batch release
```

Every step is documented in the batch record and gated by in-process quality control. The full sequence is detailed in [FICT Test Strip Production Process](/domain-11-manufacturing-production/production-process/).

## What Makes Our Manufacturing Different

- **End-to-end control** — from membrane coating to final packaging, nothing is outsourced that affects assay performance.
- **Documented traceability** — every lot traces back to its raw-material lots, equipment, and operator.
- **Lot-to-lot consistency** — process validation and in-process QC target tight CV% across batches (see [Lot-to-Lot Consistency](https://research.migibio.net/technical-reports/lot-consistency-report/)).
